# ep13: Ella Baker, Shaw University and the founding of SNCC (1960)

Category: civil rights. Made 2026-11-24 (Tuesday; Instagram + X day).

## Claims and sources (2+ each)
1. Ella Baker graduated from Shaw University (Raleigh) in 1927 as valedictorian ("top of her class").
   - BlackPast, Ella Baker (1903-1986): https://www.blackpast.org/african-american-history/baker-ella-1903-1986/
   - American Academy of Arts & Sciences / NCpedia: https://www.amacad.org/person/ella-josephine-baker ; https://www.ncpedia.org/biography/baker-ella
   - Univ. of Houston Center for Public History bio: https://uh.edu/class/ctr-public-history/tobearfruit/resources_bios_baker.html
2. Feb 1960 Greensboro sit-in by four freshmen; sit-ins spread across the South (sourced in ep06 meta.md).
   - Stanford King Institute, Sit-ins: https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/sit-ins ; NC DNCR SNCC marker: https://www.dncr.nc.gov/blog/2016/04/15/snccs-raleigh-roots
3. Baker was working for SCLC (Dr. King's group) as its executive director/secretary in 1960 (narration says only "working for").
   - King Institute, Baker, Ella Josephine: https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/baker-ella-josephine ; SNCC Digital Gateway, Ella Baker: https://snccdigital.org/people/ella-baker/
4. SCLC put up about $800 for the conference (Baker persuaded King/SCLC).
   - SNCC Digital Gateway, Ella Baker / Founding of SNCC: https://snccdigital.org/people/ella-baker/ ; https://snccdigital.org/events/founding-of-sncc/
   - Baker oral history, Documenting the American South (UNC, G-0008): https://docsouth.unc.edu/sohp/G-0008/excerpts/excerpt_8584.html ("about $800")
5. Conference at Shaw University, Raleigh, April 15-17, 1960, Easter weekend.
   - NC DNCR (state archives) SNCC marker H-107: https://www.dncr.nc.gov/blog/2023/12/28/student-nonviolent-coordinating-committee-h-107
   - King Institute, SNCC: https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/student-nonviolent-coordinating-committee-sncc ; SNCC Digital "Birth of SNCC" (Easter weekend)
   - Attendance figures disagree (126 delegates / ~150 / 200+ / 300), so the narration gives no number.
6. King hoped for an SCLC student wing; Baker urged the students to stay independent / student-directed.
   - SNCC Digital "Birth of SNCC": https://snccdigital.org/inside-sncc/the-story-of-sncc/birth-of-sncc/
   - King Institute, SNCC ("encouraged ... to remain autonomous"); NC DNCR blog "Ella Baker and SNCC": https://www.dncr.nc.gov/blog/2014/12/13/ella-baker-and-the-student-nonviolent-coordinating-committee
7. Students formed the (temporary) Student Nonviolent Coordinating Committee at the conference.
   - NC DNCR H-107; King Institute SNCC; SNCC Digital founding page.
8. SNCC joined the 1961 Freedom Rides; helped lead Mississippi voter registration including Freedom Summer 1964.
   - King Institute, SNCC and Freedom Summer: https://kinginstitute.stanford.edu/freedom-summer
   - CRMVet, Mississippi Freedom Summer 1964: https://www.crmvet.org/tim/fs.htm ; HISTORY.com Freedom Summer
9. Baker wrote that the sit-ins were about something "bigger than a hamburger" ("Bigger Than a Hamburger," Southern Patriot, 1960).
   - CRMVet text: https://www.crmvet.org/docs/sncc2.htm ; Columbia Freedom & Citizenship: https://freedomandcitizenship.columbia.edu/node/446

Sources found via web search; claims kept cautious where sources differ (title, attendance).
