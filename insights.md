## Starting assumptions (2026-10-29, from research, not our data yet)
- Post Shorts at 6 PM Central. Fridays are the strongest day for Shorts, Mondays and Tuesdays the weakest.
- Long videos: Sunday morning (9 AM Central). SocialPilot's data favours weekday 2–4 PM instead; worth testing later.
- Thumbnails: a big face showing emotion, high contrast, 3–5 words that tease rather than repeat the title.
- Hashtags: 3–5 accurate tags; keyword-rich first caption line matters more.
